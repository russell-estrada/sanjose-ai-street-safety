# Traffic Signal: AI for keeping pedestrians safe on streets

Client: City of San José, Increasing Community Safety
Team: Ahmed Al-Tameemi, Sofia Amir, Russell Estrada, Nancy Kherella, Arvin Vinluan, Aug–Oct 2026
My part: I connected the app to display public records of recent accidents as well as adding accesibility features to fill in options.

## The problem
The app stil belongs to the team and was shown off to City Officals of San Jose. It breaks when trying to give a compete report summary to the Staff.

## What we built
• Prototype collects user reports of street acccients and creates a summary of the report & shows recent accients in a chosen area.
• User sees a form to fill in the accident details & a map showcasing recent street accidents.
• Human decides what details they want to provide, what accidents they want to see & Staff decides what information is crucial in the summary.

## What I would do next
I would flesh out the Accident History page, as I want to expand what a user can see on the map and make more options on filtering accidents.
